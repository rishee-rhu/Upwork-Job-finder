FindJobs

Turns a student dossier into a short list of Upwork jobs that are open now and winnable. Claude runs on your account; jobs come from Apify. Your Upwork account is never touched.

Claude: checkingApify: checking

1Connect Apify

This page can't hold API keys (Claude pages are sandboxed), so Apify connects through your Claude account. Your key stays with Apify and Claude, never in this page.

One-time setup, about 2 minutes
  1. Open claude.ai → Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  2. Name it exactly Apify and set the URL to https://mcp.apify.com. Save.
  3. Click Connect and sign in to Apify. No key to copy; Apify's sign-in handles it. (If you prefer your API token, Apify's sign-in page accepts that too.)
  4. Come back here and press Check connection.

2Student profile none

Upload a dossier and Claude extracts the profile, or start from an example. Check evidence (what may be claimed) and weak_or_unproven (what never will be) before searching.

3Search and screen

Each query is one Apify run (they cost Apify credits). Jobs returned by a live run count as open at the time of the run. Then every job passes the gates and Claude judges fit.

Starts with the profile's own queries. Trim to the best 5-8.
